# Blog brief (from Marketing)

**Date:** 19 September 2026
**Title idea:** 5 Premium Ice Cream Trends Shaping Latin America
**Audience:** Café owners, restaurant buyers and retailers in Panama and Latin America
**Goal:** Show that premium ice cream is a growing opportunity; make readers want to add it to their menu
**Tone:** Friendly, expert, optimistic. No hard selling.
**Length:** 500–700 words

## Key points to cover
1. Consumers "trade up" to small premium treats
2. Local tropical flavours (passion fruit, guava, cacao, coffee)
3. Lighter options: less sugar, plant-based, dairy-free
4. Ice cream as an Instagram moment (colour, presentation)
5. Year-round demand in warm climates, not just summer

## Call to action
"Thinking about adding premium ice cream to your menu? Contact us for a tasting."

## Image idea
Cinematic close-up of colourful scoops of tropical gelato (passion fruit, mango, cacao)
in a glass cup on a café table, warm natural light, Panama City skyline softly blurred in the background.
